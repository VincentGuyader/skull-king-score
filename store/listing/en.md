## Full description (Play 4000 max, App Store 4000 max)

Skull King Ultimate Score is the score keeper that handles EVERY case of the Skull King card game, where other apps stop at the basic scoring table.

EVERY RULE, NOT JUST THE SCORING TABLE
- Classic or Rascal scoring (Grapeshot and Cannonball)
- Official bonuses: 14 of a suit, black 14, mermaid taken by a pirate, pirate taken by the Skull King, Skull King taken by a mermaid
- Loot, Kraken and White Whale options
- Official expansion: 19 more cards, up to 9 players, 7 and 8 bonuses, Davy Jones, First Mate Con, Mary Thorne
- Named pirates: powers recalled when you score, the Rascal's wager, Harry the Giant's bid change
- House rules: add your own cards and penalties with their points, quantity and extra tricks
- Free adjustment per player and per round for the cases nobody planned for

INPUT DESIGNED FOR THE GAME TABLE
- Bids and tricks with plus and minus counters, default value 0
- Bonus sheet per player, one tap away
- Total bid, under-bid and over-bid shown live
- Fix a round that was already validated
- Automatic number of cards per round, or your own custom sequence
- The screen stays on during the game

SCORES YOU CAN READ
- Live ranking, progress chart, round by round table
- Share the final result as a message

A HALL OF FAME THAT LASTS
- Player roster with a free colour and one of 50 icons
- Game archive, statistics per player: wins, podiums, bid accuracy, records, favourite pirate
- Head to head between players
- Enter an old game to complete the history
- Back up and restore the whole history to a file

YOUR PRIVACY, RESPECTED
- No account, no server, no ads, no tracking
- All data stays on your device
- Works offline
- Available in English, French, German and Spanish, with the terminology of the official editions

Skull King is a trademark of Grandpa Beck's Games. This app is an unofficial score keeper, not affiliated with the publisher, and reproduces none of the game's artwork. You need the card game to play.
